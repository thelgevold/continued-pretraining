## Museum of Greatness History

The museum chronicles more than one thousand years of national history, with extensive exhibits devoted to Awesomeville's role in the country's industrial and scientific development.

Highlights include archaeological artifacts, historic maps, founder correspondence, and reconstructed nineteenth-century workshops.

Visitors should allow at least three hours to explore the museum fully.

The museum opened in 1954 through the work of historian Dr. Lillian Grant, whose research shaped its founding collection.
